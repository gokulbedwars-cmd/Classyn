Why does Windows show a warning?

      
Windows shows this message for apps from publishers it does not know yet. The Classyn installer has not been signed with a paid publisher certificate, so Windows cannot tell who made it.

      
If you downloaded it from the link we sent you, it is safe to continue: click More info, then Run anyway. You can also check the file yourself, as shown further down.

Questions, answered.

    

      
Do I need an account?
Yes. Your school creates it. Log in with your school name and admission number, or with your school email. If you do not have one yet, ask your school office.

      
Does it work without internet?
No. Classyn needs an internet connection, because your attendance, homework and messages are kept online so everyone sees the same thing.

      
Why can I not download it here?
The setup file is bigger than 25 MB. That is too big to attach to an email, so we send it as a download link instead.

      
How do I update it?
Email us again for the newest link, then run the new installer. It installs over the old version and keeps you logged in.

      
How do I remove it?
Open Windows Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps. Find Classyn, click the three dots, and choose Uninstall.

      
I forgot my password.
Choose Forgot password? on the login screen and we will email you a reset link. You can also change your password from your Profile page once you are logged in.

      
Is there a version for phones?
You can use Classyn in any phone browser today. Android and iPhone apps are coming.
