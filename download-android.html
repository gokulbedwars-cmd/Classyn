Install in four steps.

It takes about a minute.

  1. Download and open

    Tap Download for Android. When it finishes, open Classyn.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder.

  2. Allow it

    Your phone will warn about installing from outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, then turn on Allow from this source, and go back.

  3. Install

    Tap Install, then wait a moment. Tap Open when it's done, or find the Classyn icon on your home screen.

  4. Log in

    Type your school name and admission number, and your password, just like on the website.

Why does my phone show a warning?

Android shows this message for any app installed from outside the Play Store, to make sure you meant to do it. The Classyn app is not on the Play Store yet.

If you downloaded it from this page, it is safe to continue: allow installs from your browser, then tap Install. You can also check the file yourself, as shown further down.

Questions, answered.

Why isn't Classyn on the Play Store?

Classyn is new. Until it is published there, this direct download is the way to install it on Android.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Your school creates it. Log in with your school name and admission number, or with your school email. If you do not have one yet, ask your school office.

Does it work without internet?

No. Classyn needs an internet connection, because your attendance, homework and messages are kept online so everyone sees the same thing.

How do I update it?

Download the newest file and open it. It installs over the old version and keeps you logged in.

How do I remove it?

Press and hold the Classyn icon, then tap Uninstall, or go to Settings, Apps, find Classyn, and tap Uninstall.

I forgot my password.

Choose Forgot password? on the login screen and we will email you a reset link. You can also change your password from your Profile page once you are logged in.
