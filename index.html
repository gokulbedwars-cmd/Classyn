    Open Classyn
    How to install Classyn on Windows
    1. Ask for the link. The setup file is bigger than 25 MB, so we send it by email. Use the Windows button above to see how to ask, then open the file we send you.
    2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This message appears for new apps that have not been signed yet.
    3. Follow the setup: Welcome, accept the License Agreement, choose where to install, then Install.
    4. Open Classyn from the Desktop or the Start menu, and log in with your school name and admission number.

    Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and an internet connection. To remove Classyn later, use Settings, Apps, Installed apps.

    Questions, answered.

    Still stuck? Ask your school office. They set up your Classyn account.

    Who can use Classyn?

    Students, teachers and admins of a school that has set it up. Accounts are created by the school, so ask your school office if you don't have one yet.

    How do I log in?

    Type your school name and the admission number your school gave you, or your school email, with your password. If you forget it, choose "Forgot password" on the login page and we'll email you a reset link.

    Do I have to install anything?

    No. Classyn works in any modern browser. The Android, iOS and Windows apps are there if you would rather have an app.

    Can other students see my results?

    No. Each student sees only their own results, attendance and class. Teachers see the classes they teach.

    Who can read my messages?

    Only you and the teacher in that conversation.

    Can I change how it looks?

    Yes. Choose from twelve color themes, in light or dark, from Settings. Your choice is remembered on your device.
